# D2P (Design-to-Product) Lab – Complete Component Master List

## Purpose

This is a **college student-level Design-to-Product component inventory** for a modern electronics, embedded systems, IoT, robotics, AI, automation and product-prototyping lab.

The focus is on **components and development modules**, not large laboratory machines.

The list is designed so students can progress from:

**Idea → Circuit → Embedded Controller → Communication → Sensors → Actuator → Prototype → Product**

> Existing inventory from the supplied lists should be retained. The items below are the recommended component-level additions and expansion stock for a high-capability D2P lab.

# 49. D2P Lab Component Philosophy

The lab should not be built only around ready-made Arduino modules.

Students should be able to choose between:

**Level 1 – Module**
> Connect a ready-made sensor/module.

**Level 2 – Embedded**
> Write firmware and integrate multiple peripherals.

**Level 3 – Product Prototype**
> Design power, communication, enclosure and PCB.

**Level 4 – Advanced Product**
> Use CAN/RS485/LoRa/BLE/Matter/AI/robotics.

**Level 5 – Product Development**
> Design a custom PCB, custom firmware, mechanical enclosure and tested working prototype.

## Procurement rule

Use **1–2 units** for expensive Level-5/R&D boards such as USRP B210, AD9361 SDR, Jetson Orin-class boards, RTK GNSS and specialized scientific sensors. Use **3–5 units** for advanced sensors and development boards used by multiple student teams. Use **5–10+ units** for common embedded modules. Mark anything already physically available as **AVAILABLE** instead of buying duplicates.
